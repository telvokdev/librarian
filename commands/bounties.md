# Bounties

Browse open bounties and manage your bounty activity.

## Action

1. Call `bounty_list()` to show open bounties.
2. Display results in a readable format (title, amount, tags, expiry).
3. Offer the user options:
   - Claim a bounty to fulfill it
   - Create a new bounty
   - View their own bounties with `my_bounties()`
